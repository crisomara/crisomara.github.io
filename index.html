About Me

I was born in Gulu, a culturally rich and historic city in the Northern region of Uganda. My academic journey led me to earn a Bachelor of Engineering degree in Biomedical and Mechatronics Engineering from Kyambogo University.

My interests center on Data Science, with a passion for model based Machine Learning and it's applicability in Healthcare and Epidemiology. I am also particularly fascinated with certain areas such as Biomedical Signal Processing, Cardiology, telemedicine and digital health. I am focused on leveraging machine learning to develop innovative solutions for healthcare challenges.

Featured and Current Projects

Wearable MI Detection System for Sub-Saharan Africa

Myocardial infarction (MI) is a critical contributor to premature death globally, with delayed diagnosis being a key challenge in Sub-Saharan Africa. This project addresses the gap by designing a low-cost, wearable system powered by machine learning to detect and monitor early signs of MI in real-time, helping to bridge the gap caused by limited infrastructure and clinical specialists.

🚀🔬 Innovation at National Science Week 2025

At National Science Week 2025, my team and I presented our AI-powered myocardial infarction detection system, demonstrating how a portable ECG device connected to a cloud-based AI model could alert healthcare providers to early heart attack risks in real time.
